# HRMS Portal user guide

Use this guide to sign in, find your account settings, and explore the portal. The guide is also available from **User guide** in the sidebar.

## Sign in

1. Open the portal home page and select **Sign in**.
2. Enter the email address and password for your staff account. Your portal administrator provisions staff accounts. If you need to set or reset your password, use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.
3. Follow the email verification prompt if it appears. If two-factor authentication is enabled for your account, follow the sign-in challenge.

## Find your way around

1. **Dashboard** shows the current time in the portal's configured time zone and shortcuts to your account and this guide.
2. **Employees** lists the profiles you may view. Open a profile to see its employee number and assignment history. Admin and HR can view profiles in their company. Managers can view their current direct reports and their own profile. Other staff can view their linked profile.
   - Admin and HR can search the directory, select **New employee**, and edit a profile's name, employee number, start date and active status. Employee numbers must be unique within the company. An inactive linked employee cannot sign in or keep using an existing session.
   - Open a profile and choose **Add assignment or transfer**. Select an active department, position and location, an optional reporting manager, and the effective start date. A transfer closes the prior open assignment on the previous day and keeps both records in the history. Use **Correct assignment** on a history entry to fix an error; the portal records the previous and replacement values for audit.
   - To give an employee portal access, open an unlinked profile, enter a unique work email and choose a role under **Invite staff account**. The portal sends a password setup link; it never displays a password. The staff member must verify their email after signing in. Admins can assign any portal role; HR cannot grant the admin role. Use **Resend password setup link** if the first message fails or expires.
   - Admin and HR can open **Organization** to search, add, rename, or deactivate departments, positions and locations. Locations require an IANA time zone such as `Asia/Dhaka`. Deactivated records remain in assignment history and cannot be selected for new assignments.
3. **My Attendance** shows your current punch state and history. Select **Check in** or **Check out** when the button is available. The portal records its own current time; you cannot enter a different time through self-service. An effective location assignment is required. A check-in left open for more than 24 hours is marked as a missing check-out, without assuming an absence or scheduled shift.
4. **Team Attendance** is available to Admin and HR for their company and to managers for current direct reports. Search or choose a date range to see effective check-in/out counts, then open an employee's history. Pending corrections do not change these counts.
   - Authorized HR/admin or a current reporting manager can make a **Manual entry** for another employee with a required reason. Enter the employee location's local date and time. Entries are limited to the previous 90 days and at most five minutes ahead by default. The portal validates the assignment location time zone. If clocks repeat an hour, provide the UTC offset (for example `-04:00`) to identify the intended instant.
   - On an original record, choose **Propose correction** to supply replacement action/time and a reason. The original remains intact. Admin or HR reviewers can approve or reject a proposal with a reason; the proposer, subject employee and original recorder cannot approve it. Approval changes the effective history after sequence validation. Rejection leaves effective history unchanged.
   - The work date of an overnight check-out stays with its check-in. History displays the stored event, source, recorder, work date and decision trail. Counts use approved corrections. The separate schedule view interprets attendance against a published duty.
5. **My schedule** shows your assigned shifts by local work date. Choose a month to inspect expected start/end times, overnight duties, scheduled work minutes after the fixed unpaid break, rest days, and location time zones. A day without an assigned shift is shown as unscheduled; it is not automatically an absence.
   - The same view compares effective attendance punches with scheduled duties. It shows worked minutes, minutes late beyond arrival grace, minutes early beyond departure grace, missing check-outs, and no-show **candidates** only after a duty and departure grace have ended. These are calculated review flags, not an HR decision or payroll deduction. Work on a rest day or without an assigned shift is labeled separately.
   - Approved full-day leave excuses a scheduled day with no punches. Pending, rejected, withdrawn and cancelled leave do not. A half-day approval is marked for review because this release does not map its morning/afternoon label to a portion of every possible shift. Recorded work during approved leave is also marked for review. Rescheduling does not change recorded punches or previously charged leave.
   - **Team schedule** lets Admin and HR view company staff and managers view only current direct reports. A manager cannot publish schedules. Open an employee to inspect the month view.
   - Admin and HR use **Shift settings** to create a company/location shift template with local start and end times, an unpaid break and separate arrival/departure grace minutes. The portal derives whether the shift ends the next day. Select explicit ISO weekdays and future effective dates to publish a schedule for an employee at the same employment location. You can use different templates on disjoint weekdays. To change an existing pattern from a future date, choose it in **Replace an existing schedule**; the prior row is closed on the previous day and an audit revision is retained. An assigned template's timing and break rules are frozen; create a new template when those rules change. Inactive templates remain in history but cannot be assigned again.
6. **My leave** shows your yearly balance by leave type: granted or adjusted, used, pending, and available days. Select a year to see another year's balance. A pending request reserves days, an approval consumes them, and a rejection or withdrawal releases the reservation. An authorized cancellation restores approved days with a separate reversal entry.
   - Choose a leave type and local start/end dates, optionally a morning or afternoon half day for one date, then submit. The system counts only the working weekdays configured for your assigned location, excluding its holiday dates. If a request spans a year boundary, each year's days use that year's balance. A single assignment must cover the entire period. Pending and approved requests cannot overlap except complementary morning/afternoon half days on one date.
   - Your history shows the status and charged days. For a pending request, enter a reason and choose **Withdraw**. Approved leave requires HR/admin cancellation; it cannot be silently deleted.
7. **Team leave** is available to Admin and HR within their company and to managers for current direct reports. The month view shows approved leave without private reasons. The list includes pending, approved, rejected, withdrawn and cancelled requests. A direct manager may approve or reject a report's pending request when its policy permits; HR/admin can also decide. The requester cannot approve their own request even if their role changes. Reviewers must enter a reason. HR/admin can cancel an approved request with a reason; the balance reversal and decision remain in the audit history.
8. **Leave settings** is available to Admin and HR. Add leave types, then create dated policies for each type and location. Select working weekdays explicitly; the portal has no legal or weekend defaults. Choose the annual allowance in half-day units (for example, `40` means 20 days), whether half days and negative balances are allowed, and whether direct managers may approve. Effective policy date ranges for the same type and location cannot overlap. A new version with a later start automatically closes an open-ended prior version on the preceding day. Existing versions remain available for historical requests.
   - Add location holidays by date. Grant each employee their annual allocation with a reason. Ordinary yearly grants cannot exceed the policy allowance; use a justified **Adjustment** for an exception or correction. Positive and negative adjustments are recorded as separate ledger entries. A negative adjustment cannot spend already reserved or used balance. There is no automatic accrual or carry forward in this release; grant each year's opening balance explicitly.
   - Policy and holiday changes affect new requests. Already submitted requests keep their calculated charge and policy reference. Paid/unpaid is a label only and does not calculate payroll.
9. **Profile** lets you update your display name and email. An email change requires verification of the new address.
10. **Security** lets you change your password and set up two-factor authentication. Enter your current password when prompted.
11. **Appearance** lets you select Light, Dark, or System. On a small screen, open the sidebar using the menu button at the top.
12. **Portal home** takes you to the public landing page. Use **Log out** in the account menu to end your session.

## Payroll MVP

### Bangladesh Compliance review workspace

Admin and HR can open **Bangladesh Compliance** to inspect official source links and create an effective-dated draft rules pack. Each candidate rule starts with unresolved applicability and legal interpretation. HR may record a provisional employee category and establishment regime with a reason and effective window; these labels require qualified review and do not change pay. Submit a pack for legal review to preserve its draft and audit history. Preview an existing run to compare its generic gross, deductions and net with the pending statutory estimate. **Not calculated** means no statutory amount has been established; it does not mean zero tax. Attestation and activation stay blocked until every applicable rule has independently verified source interpretation and signed cases. Current deployments have statutory activation disabled, and existing payslips stay unchanged.

On a pack page, use the **Review readiness checklist** to see which preparation steps remain open. To register a reviewer document, choose its evidence type, enter a private document reference and the SHA-256 digest of the exact archived file, and optionally link the matching candidate rule and official source. A submitted entry is permanently labeled `supplied_unverified` and appears in the audit history. Registration alone does not verify a legal conclusion, permit attestation, or produce a statutory estimate. Keep signed source copies and the qualified reviewer worksheet in access-controlled private storage; the portal stores only metadata.

For cPanel deployment, run the ordinary Laravel migration after a backup. This adds compliance-only tables and a small official-source register; it does not alter existing payroll tables, require a queue worker, or call an outside service during payroll calculation. For a statutory update, collect the official amended text, document publication and operative dates separately, obtain signed qualified example calculations, create a new pack version, and review it independently before any controlled rollout. Never edit a finalized run to apply a new rule.

### Staging and account onboarding

An operator can run the read-only `php artisan hrms:preflight` command after deploying and migrating a staging release. Resolve every **BLOCK** before promotion. The command checks configuration, PHP extensions, built assets, writable runtime directories, database access and pending migrations without printing secret values. It does not test the host's public document root, actual email delivery or a backup restore; use the [deployment runbook](../operations/deployment.md) for those gates.

For fictional staging, provision an Admin from a trusted terminal, then invite HR and staff from the employee page. A new staff member uses the emailed reset link to set a password, signs in and verifies their email. The reset link expires after 60 minutes, is single use, and a resend is throttled for 60 seconds. If delivery fails, the linked account remains unverified; an authorized Admin or HR can retry from the employee page after mail is repaired. Create organization, reporting assignments, shifts and company-approved leave policies before starting attendance. Payroll, payslip and payment demonstrations must use fictional amounts only.

Payroll stores money as integer minor units. For BDT, enter poisha: `3100000` means BDT 31,000.00. The portal does not calculate statutory tax, legal overtime rates or transfer money. Have local payroll and legal advisers review your configured policy and each manual withholding or overtime decision before production use.

1. Admin or HR opens **Payroll**. Create a dated policy with currency BDT and a documented proration basis. Calendar-day proration divides monthly salary by the number of days in that month; scheduled-day proration divides by published scheduled days. Keep effective policy versions separate without overlapping dates.
2. Open an employee under **Compensation**. Enter a dated monthly base amount, optional fixed monthly allowance and deduction components, and a reason. Save later salary changes as a new non-overlapping version. The run uses the version effective on each eligible work date. Employment assignment dates limit payable days; approved paid leave does not automatically reduce salary.
3. Create a **regular** monthly run and select employees. Each company/month has one regular run. Reopening its creation form with different employees or a different preparer is rejected; open the existing run instead. For a correction to a finalized month, create a **supplemental** run with a reason and select the affected staff; it contains only new corrections, not the original base pay.
   If the form reports a validation error, the message receives keyboard focus above the form. Review it, then correct the month or employee selection and submit again.
4. Inspect **Attendance exceptions** in the run. Missing punches, candidate no-shows, unusual schedules, partial or unpaid leave and possible overtime require HR/admin financial review before approval. Mark a date **Neutral** with a reason for no pay change, or enter an explicit positive poisha amount for **Approved overtime** or **Approved unpaid**. Attendance flags never dock pay or grant overtime automatically. A manager can inspect direct-report facts under **Attendance exceptions** in the sidebar and send a factual recommendation, but cannot view salary or make the financial decision.
5. Add a one-time bonus, deduction, explicitly reviewed manual tax withholding, advance recovery, or correction with a reason. Choose **Calculate / recalculate draft**. Inspect each employee's source-labelled lines, gross, deductions and net. A new review or adjustment makes the prior calculation stale; recalculate before approval. Deductions cannot exceed earnings.
6. After the month ends and all exceptions are resolved, a **different** HR/admin user opens the run and chooses **Approve and finalize**. The preparer cannot approve their own run. Finalized amounts and line snapshots cannot be edited. Choose **Release payslips** when ready. Employees can see only their own released statement under **My payslips** and can print it.
7. HR/admin can record a manual payment amount, date and unique reference against each finalized entry. This is bookkeeping only; it does not send funds. Partial payments show an outstanding balance. Download the restricted CSV register for reconciliation. A repeated submission with the same key does not add another payment.

If a browser retry repeats a payroll review, adjustment, or payment, the portal accepts it only when the actor and details match the original submission. A changed amount, date, reference, decision, employee, or reason needs a new reviewed action; reusing the old retry key cannot silently change a financial record.

If attendance or leave facts change after a financial review, the run marks that date for review again. In a draft run, use **Save audited revision** on the existing review with the updated financial decision and a reason for revising it, then recalculate the draft. Finalized decisions and payslip amounts cannot be revised; use a supplemental run for a later correction. Spreadsheet downloads prefix formula-like employee names and numbers with an apostrophe so spreadsheet programs treat them as text.

For a fictional local walkthrough, configure an October policy, salary and allowance for one employee, create the October regular run, review any flagged attendance dates with written reasons, calculate, sign in as a second HR/admin to finalize, release, then record a manual partial payment. Use **My payslips** as that employee to inspect the released statement. Do not enter real salary or payment references in a shared demo.

The operator may seed a fictional BDT policy, compensation for Ayesha, and an October draft using `php artisan db:seed --class=PayrollDemoSeeder --force` against the isolated local demo database. The seeder refuses to run outside a local environment and can be rerun safely. October has no published demo shifts, so the draft is useful for exploring calculation, separate approval, release and manual payment. To see attendance review flags, create a run for a completed month with published shifts and recorded exceptions.

## Explore a local demo

Use the fictional account and temporary password supplied by the demo operator. Sign out before trying a different role. The employee directory, attendance, leave and schedule views change according to your role and reporting relationship. In the local demo, try **Shift settings** as HR to inspect three fictional templates and schedules; **My schedule** as Ayesha to see the Dhaka workweek and approved-leave exemptions on November 9–10; **Team schedule** as Imran to inspect Ayesha's schedule; and **My leave** as Ayesha to inspect balances and requests. The guide is available inside the portal at **User guide**.

The local demo uses pre-verified addresses and simulated mail delivery. Keep the demo email address as provided to continue exploring.

The leave demo has two fictional leave types, three location-specific policies (Dhaka and New York working weeks differ), two fictional holidays, opening balances, and approved, pending and rejected requests. The fictional unlinked New York employee demonstrates that staff records do not require a portal login. The operator can rerun `php artisan db:seed --class=LeaveDemoSeeder --force` against the isolated local demo database; this seeder refuses to run outside a local environment and is safe to rerun.

The shift demo adds published Dhaka and New York schedules without adding passwords or changing leave balances. The operator can rerun `php artisan db:seed --class=ShiftDemoSeeder --force` against that same isolated local database. The shift seeder also refuses to run outside a local environment.

## Testing the portal locally

Developers and reviewers can run the fictional browser suite with `npm run test:e2e` after installing PHP/Composer dependencies, Node dependencies and Playwright Chromium. The test runner creates a separate temporary database, captured test email log, and Admin/HR/Manager/Employee accounts for two fictional companies. It deletes the temporary run on completion. See `docs/operations/playwright-e2e.md` in the repository for setup and the coverage backlog. These accounts are only for the temporary test run; they are not portal demonstration accounts.
